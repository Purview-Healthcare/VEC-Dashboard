VEC Dashboard: end-to-end test report

9 November 2026 · version live at purview-healthcare.github.io/VEC-Dashboard · tested with the 8 reports from OneDrive_2026-11-05 plus hand-built test files

Summary

I tested the whole app and found 9 calculation bugs plus 24 other problems with matching, file reading and the screens. All are fixed and live at purview-healthcare.github.io/VEC-Dashboard, and every check passes now.

The calculation errors that affected billing (Barti, all months)

BeforeAfter

  • Insurance overpaying its share was billed to the patient. On 406 invoices where insurance paid more than its expected share, the extra was added to the patient's balance. For example, insurance paid $200 on a $200 bill, and the patient was still shown owing $80 and listed for statements.
  • Refunds were subtracted twice. On 5 invoices the payment report had already left the refunded money out, so the patient was shown owing up to $620 extra.

Charges, adjustments and payments were correct before and are unchanged. If patient balances were taken from this dashboard before 9 November 2026, re-check them on the current version before billing.

Other important fixes

  • AR notes under the wrong patient. 97 log entries had a fee slip number belonging to someone else. In the AR master log, some rows had the slip and MRN of the next row. These notes now attach only when the patient name matches, and the mismatches are listed under Biller insights so the logs can be corrected.
  • Wrong biller status. "Claim Denied" + "Closed" showed as Claim paid, and COB forwards showed as Patient responsibility. There is now an "Underpaid" category, and each patient shows their most urgent status.
  • Lost or misplaced amounts. Office Mate continuation lines were dropped. Afternoon Excel times moved to the next day. A typo year (2062) created a fake month. Accounting-format amounts like "$ (1,200.00)" read as $0.

How it was tested

  • Independent recalculation: a separate program written from scratch, sharing no code with the app, recalculated all 38,499 invoices, every month and patient in both views, and 50 filter combinations. There are zero differences.
  • Hand-built test files: 237 checks with answers worked out by hand. They all pass; the old version failed 72 of them.
  • Click-throughs: 80 checks covering every button, filter, export and upload path, including the summary at the top of the page and of every CSV export. All pass.
  • Summary on the real reports: under all 50 filter combinations, the summary's numbers match the tiles, the table and the CSV exports to the cent.
  • Second review: an independent reviewer read all the code and reported 30 findings. Every real issue it found is fixed.

Things to know

  • Office Mate has no patient side. Patient payments and balances for January 2025 – April 2026 show as n/a.
  • Barti's "Insurance Paid" column shows $14,228.48 that never arrived in the payment report, across 188 invoices. Those stay pending until the adjustment is posted in Barti.
  • Match the export dates. Payment reports should cover the same dates as the charge report.

1. How it was tested

  • Independent recalculation. A separate program, written from scratch and sharing no code with the dashboard, read the raw reports and recalculated every invoice, month and patient. It was compared with the dashboard field by field: charges, adjustments, discounts, insurance paid, patient paid, pending with insurance, pending with patient, total pending, patient counts and status counts. This was done in both month views and under 25 filters each (location, system, every payment status, date-of-service ranges, month ranges, search, biller status, and combinations).
  • Hand-built test files. Small Barti, Office Mate, payment and worklog files were made with one row per edge case: refunds, insurance overpaying its share, credits, a credit line posted a month later, a typo year, an old-format payment file, Office Mate continuation lines, notes with the wrong patient, multi-slip cells, and more. The expected answer for each was worked out by hand; there are 237 checks in all.
  • Click-throughs. Every button, filter, chip, toggle, export and upload path was scripted, and the result checked on screen and in the exported files.
  • Independent code review. A second reviewer read all of the code and reported 30 findings. Each was checked and fixed where it was real.
  • Raw-file checks. The report totals were re-added outside the browser. The charge report's own arithmetic was checked on every row: Total = Subtotal − Discounts + Taxes; Balance = Total − Adjustments; Patient + Insurance Resp. = Balance; Paid − Refunds + Insurance Paid = Total Paid. Every row passes.

Reading files, screens and exports

  • Office Mate slips with no insurance balance showed "Paid in full" even though that report has no patient payments. They now show "Insurance settled (Office Mate)".
  • Dropping the same file again after its payer types changed could double count. Re-dropping a file now replaces everything from it.
  • One blank Payer cell could make a payment file "mixed", so it replaced the other payment file. Blank rows now take the file's type.
  • Workbook sheets that weren't recognized were dropped silently. They are now reported.
  • Adding a newer export with more months didn't widen the month range. The full range now grows, and a range you narrowed is kept.
  • Worklists that need a payment report showed every claim as unpaid when that report was missing. They are now hidden, and the banner explains why.
  • New suggestion when payment reports stop before the charges do.
  • The takeback suggestion counted the original payment as its own repayment. Only a payment on or after the takeback counts now.
  • Dropping a file outside the drop area opened the file and lost the page. Files can now be dropped anywhere.
  • "Clear all" kept the old search, location and date filters. It now resets them.
  • A month or date range typed backwards showed nothing. It is now swapped.
  • A month opened by a search couldn't be collapsed.
  • Clicking the "Group months by" text switched the view.
  • Locations with doubled spaces couldn't be filtered.
  • CSV exports: cells starting with = + - @ are protected from running as Excel formulas, and line breaks are quoted.
  • Only https links from the reports become clickable.
  • Chart labels: $1,500 showed as "$2K".
  • Empty Office Mate rows (no slip and no amounts) counted as patients.
  • Placeholder keys for lines without an invoice number could collide across sheets.

5. How each number is calculated

FigureRule
Charges billedBarti "Total" (already after discounts). Office Mate: Ins Chg + Pat Chg.
AdjustmentsBarti "Adjustments". Office Mate: the first "Ins Bal" column, which holds the insurance write-offs.
Insurance / patient paidMoney in the Barti insurance and patient payment reports. Office Mate: Ins Pmt. Barti's own "Insurance Paid" column is only cross-checked, never counted as money received.
Owed on an invoiceCharges − adjustments − insurance paid − (patient paid − refunds still in the payment report).
Pending with insuranceBarti: Insurance Resp. − insurance paid, never below zero. Office Mate: its insurance balance (the second "Ins Bal").
Pending with patientOwed − pending with insurance. Negative means a patient credit. Not available for Office Mate (shown as n/a, never $0).
MonthPosting view: charge date and payment date. Date-of-service view: the visit's earliest service date, for its charges and payments. Office Mate is placed by service date in both views.
StatusUnpaid / Partially paid / Paid in full / Overpaid-credit / No charge / Payment with no charge. Office Mate slips with no insurance balance show as "Insurance settled".
DuplicatesAn Office Mate slip with the same patient and service date as a Barti invoice is left out of every total and listed under Biller insights.

6. Still worth knowing

  • Office Mate has no patient side. Patient payments and patient balances for January 2025 – April 2026 aren't in that report, so they show as n/a.
  • Barti's "Insurance Paid" column disagrees with the payment report on 188 invoices ($14,228.48). Barti shows money that never came in, usually claims closed with an adjustment. The dashboard counts only real payments, so those invoices still show as pending until the adjustment is posted.
  • Export payments for the same dates as charges. The current payment files start 31 Mar and end 30 Oct, while charges run 23 Mar – 30 Sep. The dashboard flags both gaps.
  • Fix the 97 worklog entries whose slip number belongs to another patient (list under Biller insights). Until then those notes don't show under any patient.
  • Duplicate detection is by name and date. A misspelled name in one system means the visit isn't recognized as a duplicate. Check the "Office Mate slips that also exist in Barti" list after each upload.
